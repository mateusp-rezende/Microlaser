## Brand & Style

This design system embodies surgical precision, optical physics, and uncompromising clinical engineering for medical laser technology. Designed for biomedical engineers, hospital procurement committees, chief surgeons, and operating theater directors, the interface projects absolute clinical authority, absolute sterility, and advanced photonic science.

The visual direction merges **Clinical Minimalism** with **Precision Optical High-Tech**:
- **Sterile Authority:** Foundations rely on expansive, calm clinical planes that reflect hospital sanitary standards and surgical lighting conditions.
- **Photonics & Coherence:** Accent elements reflect laser physics—coherent light waves, focused beam points, and calibrated frequency pulses rendered through controlled luminous gradients and microscopic hairline markers.
- **Precision Engineering:** Information architecture prioritizes telemetry, exact physical tolerances, and high-density technical specs without clutter. Interactive states provide immediate tactile feedback reminiscent of physical medical console interfaces.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture mirrors high-end surgical suite instrumentation:

- **Primary (`#047857` / Deep Emerald):** Represents optical stability, regulatory trust, and surgical precision. Used for structural primary actions, main navigation bars, active clinical states, and authoritative typography.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` / Tech Emerald):** The laser spectrum accent. Denotes photonic energy, live equipment statuses, calibrated laser pulses, primary call-to-actions, and focused visual feedback.
- **Tertiary (`#0F172A` / Dark Slate):** Anchors deep contrast consoles, technical telemetry readouts, dark-mode technical overlays, and surgical display docks.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:**
  - `Clinical Off-White` (`#F8FAFC`): The primary hospital ambient canvas, preventing sterile glare while preserving absolute cleanliness.
  - `Crisp White` (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated instrument cards, active data containers, and isolated interactive components.
  - `Border / Hairline Slate` (`#E2E8F0`): Clean, calibrated division lines reflecting physical equipment seams.
- **State Semantics:**
  - Calibration / Active Emission: `#10B981`
  - System Alert / Optical Warning: `#F59E0B`
  - Hazard / E-Stop / Critical: `#EF4444`
  - Laser Standby / Telemetry Idle: `#0F172A`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for modern architectural balance with **Inter** for clinical data density and zero-ambiguity optical clarity.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides clean, confident geometries with subtle sculpturing. Letter spacing tightens moderately in large hero configurations to convey structural density and poise.
- **Body & Long-Form Reading:** Inter delivers neutral, high-legibility clarity across multi-column medical specs, certification documents, and procurement catalogs.
- **Technical Readouts & Specifications:** Micro-labels and numeric telemetry leverage Inter with tabular numeric alignments, increased tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`), and uppercase capitalization to evoke calibrated medical hardware panel engraving.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an uncompromising 8pt baseline rhythm, engineered around modular medical cabinet proportions:

- **Grid Structure:**
  - **Desktop (≥1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, capped at `1440px` max content container.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.
- **Spacing Rhythm:**
  - High breathing room (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) is reserved for top-tier hospital apparatus showcase sections to project clinical calmness.
  - Component internals strictly utilize compact, rational scale increments (`space-xs` through `space-lg`) to preserve information density within telemetry dashboards and diagnostic summaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Subtle Optical Dispersion** and **Calibrated Physical Layering** rather than heavy muddy dropshadows:

1. **Base Sterile Surface:** Crisp hospital white (`#FFFFFF`) sitting on clinical off-white canvas (`#F8FAFC`), bound by `1px` crisp hairline borders in `#E2E8F0`.
2. **Elevated Medical Cards:** Low-altitude ambient shadow tinted slightly with emerald-slate:
   - `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(4, 120, 87, 0.05)`
3. **Glassmorphic Optical Overlays (Telemetry Badges & Scopes):**
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(180%)`.
   - Perimeter: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` inner border and `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` outer rim.
4. **Dark High-Contrast Optical Consoles:**
   - Background: Solid `#0F172A`.
   - Internal glow: `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 0%, rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12) 0%, transparent 70%)`.
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Soft Geometric Contours (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

- Medical grade equipment requires surgical precision; excessive pill shapes degrade authoritative engineering credibility, whereas purely sharp edges evoke legacy unergonomic software.
- Standard interactive elements (buttons, form inputs, technical pills) use `0.25rem` (4px).
- Structural hardware cards and interactive modal windows scale to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Badges and micro-status tags use a dedicated precision capsule geometry with `0.25rem` or full radius where optical beam indicator dots are housed.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Optical CTA:** Solid Tech Emerald (`#10B981`) text in `#FFFFFF`, shifting to `#059669` on hover. Accompanied by a crisp `1px` subtle highlight border `rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`. Internal padding: `12px 24px`. Letter spacing: `-0.01em`. Font: `Inter` SemiBold.
- **Secondary Clinical Button:** Pure white surface, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text in Deep Slate (`#0F172A`). Hover transitions border to Deep Emerald (`#047857`) with a faint tinted background wash (`#F0FDF4`).
- **Dark Console Button:** Dark Slate background (`#1E293B`), text in Crisp White, hover accented by a fine `#10B981` perimeter stroke.

### Laser & Pulse Status Chips
- Badges combine a glassmorphic background (`rgba(255,255,255,0.85)` or dark `rgba(15,23,42,0.8)` on dark consoles) with an active pulse indicator: a 6px circular dot radiating a continuous `@keyframes` ping in `#10B981`.
- Font: `technical-mono` uppercase with `letter-spacing: 0.08em`.

### Equipment & Telemetry Cards
- Built with a dual-accent structure: `Crisp White` body, segmented top header containing equipment model numbering (e.g., `ML-CO2-SURGICAL-V4`), laser classification labels (`CLASS IV LASER`), and an ultra-fine 1px top highlight line illuminated in `#10B981`.
- Interactive cards feature a micro-hover translation (`-2px` vertical elevation shift) with shadow expansion tinted in emerald.

### Medical Inputs & Calibration Fields
- Base state: Crisp `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, `4px` corner radius.
- Active/Focus state: Strict optical focus ring—`border-color: #10B981`, with `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`. No default browser outlines.
- Numeric parameters include dedicated engineering units affixed right (`nm`, `mJ`, `Hz`, `W`) in muted slate typography.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Switch Controls
- Custom square-soft selectors (`4px` radius).
- Checkbox active state: `#047857` fill with clinical sharp `#FFFFFF` checkmark check vector.
- Optical Toggle Switch: Precision track in `#E2E8F0` (inactive) to `#10B981` (emission active), thumb containing a subtle 1px center hairline accent.

### Additional B2B Medical Components
- **Laser Wavelength Diagram Rails:** Horizontal micro-charts mapping optical spectrum absorption curves.
- **Hospital Spec Data Matrix:** High-density tabular layout with alternating rows in `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`, right-aligned floating decimals, and compliance certification checkmarks (CE, ANVISA, FDA).
